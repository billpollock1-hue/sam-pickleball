How to Earn a Bear
🥒Pickle bear — win a game 11-0. Every player on the winning team earns one.
🏆Shootout bear (women) — finish on top of your 4-player pool (most game wins, ties broken by point differential). Any win count qualifies.
🏆Shootout bear (men) — finish on top of your pool by winning all 3 games. Leading with only 2 wins does not earn a bear (although it is tracked).
